## The characters

The nine are the AI girls of the Bilibili community: AI models drawn as anime girls. This artwork is one fan's version of them and claims no ownership of the characters.

- Mademoiselle Claude (Miss Claude) is a community character from the Bilibili community.
- The designs of the others follow ZipZipPipe's series 《大 AI 与小 AI 们》 (https://space.bilibili.com/4168597), shared under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. DeepSeek's whale girl is 上善无形's original character, in ZipZipPipe's redesign.
- The scenes were painted with reference to the 3D figures of those designs made by ApprenTice (https://space.bilibili.com/634972797), also shared under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.
